# CYP2C9-Research
Bioinformatics and pharmacogenetic research on CYP2C9

# CYP2C9 Research Protocol

## 1. General objective

Integrate genetic, molecular, pharmacological and statistical evidence to characterize CYP2C9 through reproducible bioinformatics analyses.

## 2. Research objectives

- Characterize CYP2C9 genetic variants.
- Analyze CYP2C9 protein interaction networks.
- Evaluate gene expression across tissues.
- Identify functional pathways using GO and KEGG.
- Analyze pharmacogenetic associations.
- Investigate regulatory ncRNAs and DNA methylation.
- Evaluate the feasibility of systematic reviews and meta-analyses.

## 5. Statistical analysis

- Allele and genotype frequencies.
- Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium when appropriate.
- Association analysis.
- Normality assessment when relevant.
- Heterogeneity analysis.
- Meta-analysis when data are sufficiently comparable.

## 6. Reproducibility

All datasets, scripts, statistical procedures and figures must be documented.

No results will be reported without verifiable source data.
